Privacy Policy

How Hourvia collects, uses and looks after your information.

Hourvia (“we”, “us”, “our”) publishes hourvia.com, a watch site about choosing and wearing a wristwatch: case sizes, straps, styles and time zones. This policy explains what personal information we collect, why we collect it and the choices you have.

00:00

Information you give us

Our contact form does not store anything on our website. When you press “Send message”, it opens your own email app with your name, email address, topic and message filled in, and nothing is sent until you choose to send that email. When you email or call us, or ask to join The Hourvia Dispatch by email, we receive the details you include. We use this information only to reply to you or to send the newsletter. We never ask for payment details because we sell nothing.

We ask for as little as possible. There is no account to create on hourvia.com, no password to store and no profile kept about you, so the only personal information we ever hold is what you choose to put in an email or type into the live chat. If you write to us about a correction, we keep the message only while we are dealing with it and for a short period afterwards in case the same point comes up again. You can ask us at any time what we hold about you, and we will tell you in plain language rather than sending a database export.

01:00

Information collected automatically

We automatically receive technical data such as IP address, browser and device type, operating system, referring page, pages visited and the time of your visit. The world time compare tool and wrist fit finder run entirely in your browser; your selections are not sent to us. See our Cookie Policy for cookie details.

This technical data arrives with every request your browser makes and is normal for any website on the internet. We use it in aggregate, to see which guides are read and whether anything is broken, not to identify you. We do not attempt to link it to your name or email address, and we do not buy information about you from anybody else. Where a third-party service such as our live chat or an advertising partner collects its own technical data, that happens under its own policy, which you can read on that provider's own website.

02:00

How we use information

  • To respond to questions and corrections.
  • To send the newsletter to subscribers.
  • To understand which guides are useful and improve them.
  • To protect the website from spam and misuse.
  • To meet legal obligations.

We do not use your information for anything you would not expect from a website like this one. There is no profiling, no scoring and no automated decision that affects you. We do not send marketing email to people who have not asked for it, and the only regular email we send is the newsletter you can join and leave whenever you like. If we ever wanted to use your information for a new purpose, we would ask you first rather than quietly updating this page.

03:00

Live chat (Tawk.to)

We use Tawk.to to run the chat widget in the corner of the page. If you start a chat, Tawk.to stores the messages you send, your approximate location and basic device information so we can reply, and processes that data under the Tawk.to Privacy Policy. You can browse the whole website without using the chat.

The chat widget only loads when the page loads, and it does nothing until you open it and type. If you never use it, no conversation is created and nothing about you is stored by it beyond the ordinary technical data any script receives. When you do start a chat, please keep it to the question you are asking and avoid sending anything sensitive, because the transcript sits with the chat provider as well as with us. You can ask us to delete a transcript at any time and we will do it, usually the same day.

04:00

Advertising

This website may display ads served by Google and other third-party vendors. These vendors use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this website or other websites. Google’s use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads based on your visits to this and other sites. You can opt out of personalised advertising at Google Ads Settings or www.aboutads.info/choices.

We do not control which ads appear, and seeing an ad on this website is not a recommendation from us. If you would rather not see personalised advertising, the two links above let you turn it off at the source, and choosing “Essential only” in our cookie notice stops non-essential cookies being set from this website. Ads are kept clearly separate from our own writing, and no advertiser is shown an article before it is published or allowed to influence what it says.

05:00

Sharing

We do not sell or rent personal information. We share it only with service providers that help us run the website, such as hosting and email delivery, or where the law requires.

The providers we use are limited to the ones a small website genuinely needs: hosting, email delivery and the live chat widget. Each of them is only given what it needs to do its job, and none of them is allowed to use your information for their own marketing. We have never sold personal information and we have no plans to. If we were ever required to hand something over by a court or a regulator, we would comply, and we would tell you unless the law stopped us from doing so.

06:00

Retention

Messages are kept only as long as needed to respond. Newsletter addresses are kept until you unsubscribe. Chat transcripts are kept for as long as the conversation may still be useful, and you can ask us to delete yours at any time.

In practice this means most messages are deleted within a few months, and nothing is kept “just in case”. Newsletter addresses stay only while you are subscribed, and unsubscribing removes yours from the list rather than flagging it as inactive. If you ask us to delete something, we do it and confirm by email once it is done. Backups may keep a copy for a short period after deletion, but those are overwritten on a rolling basis and are never used to restore an address you have removed.

07:00

Your rights

Depending on where you live, including under the GDPR and CCPA, you may have the right to access, correct, delete or restrict the use of your personal information. Contact us to make a request. Every newsletter includes an unsubscribe link.

You do not need to use any particular form of words to exercise these rights, and we will not charge you for a reasonable request. We normally reply within a few days and complete requests within one month. We may ask you to confirm the email address you used with us, simply so that we do not hand somebody else your information by mistake. If we cannot do what you have asked, for example because we no longer hold anything about you, we will say so clearly and explain why.

08:00

Children

This website is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their personal information. Please contact us if you believe a child has provided personal data.

The guides and tools on hourvia.com are written for a general adult audience, and nothing here is aimed at children or designed to appeal to them. We do not ask anybody for their age, and we have no interest in collecting information about young people. If you are a parent or guardian and you believe a child has sent us their details, write to hello@hourvia.com and we will remove them promptly and confirm when it is done, without needing a formal request.

09:00

Security

We use reasonable safeguards, including HTTPS encryption. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

The website is served over an encrypted connection, and the small amount of information we do hold sits in ordinary business email and chat accounts protected by strong passwords and two-factor sign-in. Because we take no payments and store no card details, there is very little here for anybody to steal. No system is completely secure, so please do not send us anything confidential by email or chat. If something ever did go wrong, we would tell affected people and the relevant authority as quickly as the law requires.

10:00

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The date above shows the current version.

Any new version is published on this page with a fresh date at the top, so you can always see when it last moved. Where a change materially affects you, we will make that clear on the page rather than hiding it in a paragraph. Continuing to use hourvia.com after a change means you accept the updated version. We do not keep a public archive of older versions, so if you need a copy of the version that applied on a particular date, ask us and we will send it to you.

11:00

Legal bases for using your information

Where data protection law in the UK or the European Economic Area applies, we rely on three legal bases. Consent covers newsletter sign-ups and any non-essential cookies, and you can withdraw it at any time. Legitimate interests cover replying to your messages, keeping the website secure and understanding which guides readers find useful. Legal obligation covers anything we are required to keep by law.

We do not rely on consent for anything that would still work without it, and we do not rely on legitimate interests to do something you would find surprising. If you withdraw consent, for example by unsubscribing, that does not make anything we did beforehand unlawful; it simply stops us doing it from that point. Where you think we have the balance wrong, tell us and we will look at it properly rather than defending the position by default.

12:00

Where your information is held

Our hosting, email and live chat providers may store or process information on servers outside your own country, including in the United States. Where information leaves the UK or the European Economic Area, we rely on the standard contractual clauses or the equivalent safeguards published by those providers.

We have deliberately kept the number of providers small, because every extra service is another place your information could sit. None of them is used for marketing and none is given more than it needs. If you would like to know which providers are involved at the moment, write to us and we will list them. Should we ever move to a different provider in another country, we would check the safeguards before the move rather than afterwards.

13:00

Do Not Track and Global Privacy Control

Browsers send several different privacy signals and there is no single agreed standard for them. Where your local law requires it, we treat a Global Privacy Control signal as an opt-out of non-essential cookies. You can also choose “Essential only” in our cookie notice at any time, on any page.

In practice the cookie notice is the control that actually does something on this website, because it is ours rather than a request to a third party. Choosing “Essential only” stops non-essential cookies being set from here, and clearing this site’s data in your browser resets the choice so you can make it again. We do not treat a privacy signal as a reason to show you a worse version of the website, and nothing on hourvia.com is withheld from people who decline cookies.

14:00

If you are in California

We do not sell personal information, and we do not share it for cross-context behavioural advertising, as those terms are defined under California law. We do not offer rewards or discounts in exchange for your information. You may ask us what we hold, ask us to delete it, or ask us to correct it, and we will not treat you differently for asking.

You may also ask somebody else to make a request on your behalf, and we will deal with it in the same way once we are satisfied they are acting for you. There is no charge, there is no form to fill in, and we will not ask you to create an account to exercise a right. If we have to refuse a request, for example because it would reveal somebody else’s information, we will say so and explain the reason rather than simply going quiet.

15:00

Automated decisions

We do not make automated decisions that have a legal or similarly significant effect on you. The interactive tools on hourvia.com work out their results inside your own browser; your entries are not sent to us and are not used to build a profile of you.

We also do not use your information to train models, score you, or decide what version of a page you see. Everybody gets the same guides in the same order. The only thing remembered between visits is the cookie choice you made, which stays in your own browser and is never read by us. If that ever changed, we would say so on this page before it happened, not after.

16:00

How to complain

If you think we have handled your information badly, please tell us first at hello@hourvia.com so that we can put it right. You also have the right to complain to the data protection authority where you live.

We would always rather hear from you directly first, because most problems are a misunderstanding we can clear up the same day. Tell us what happened and what you would like us to do about it, and we will reply with a plain answer rather than a form letter. If our answer does not satisfy you, you can take it to the data protection authority where you live, and you do not need our permission or involvement to do so.
